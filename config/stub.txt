read startDistance:int
gameloop
loop 2 read hp:int stamina:int moveName:word(16) frame:int rise:int roundKick:int
loopline 38 x:int y:int vx:int vy:int apart:int angle:float target:float strength:int
read sinceContact:int sinceHit:int
write STANCE

STATEMENT
Drive a fighter: land strikes, keep the balance and stay in the ring.

INPUT
startDistance: distance between the two pelvises at the start, in mm
hp: HP of a fighter (first line: the player's fighter, second line: the opponent)
stamina: stamina of the fighter, in thousandths of a percent (100000 = 100 %)
moveName: the move the fighter is in: a move name, or JOINTS
frame: how many turns of that move have been played (0 for a held pose)
rise: how deep the fighter is ducked, in quarters (0 to 4)
roundKick: 1 when the fighter's lead leg went around the other body last turn (round kick), else 0
x: x of a point, in mm from the left of the scene (all on one line: the 19 points of the player's fighter, then the opponent's, in the order of the statement)
y: y of the point, in mm down from the top of the scene (the floor is at y = 3000)
vx: horizontal speed of the point, in mm/s
vy: vertical speed of the point, in mm/s (positive: downward)
apart: physics steps since the striking part at this point last touched the other fighter (0 to 10; 10 for the points that do not strike)
angle: angle of the joint that moves this point, in degrees (0 when no joint moves it)
target: target angle of that joint's motor, in degrees
strength: strength of that joint's motor, in %
sinceContact: turns since the fighters last touched (0: during the last turn)
sinceHit: turns since a strike last took HP (0: during the last turn)

OUTPUT
A command: a move (STANCE, GUARD, RELAX, DUCK, JAB, CROSS, OVERHAND, KICK, HIGH_KICK, STEP_FORWARD, STEP_BACK, JUMP), WAIT, or JOINTS from the second league; optionally followed by a message.
