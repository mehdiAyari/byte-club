read myMove:word(16) myFrame:int oppMove:word(16) oppFrame:int
loop 19 read x:int y:int vx:int vy:int

loopline 12 apart:int
write STANCE

STATEMENT
Drive your fighter: land strikes, keep your balance and stay in the ring.

INPUT
myId: 0 if you are on the left (facing right), 1 on the right (facing left)
startDistance: distance between the two pelvises at the start, in mm
myHp: your HP
myStamina: your stamina, in thousandths of a percent (100000 = 100 %)
oppHp: your opponent's HP
oppStamina: your opponent's stamina
myMove: the move you are in: a move name, or JOINTS
myFrame: how many turns of that move have been played (0 for a held pose)
oppMove: the move your opponent is in
oppFrame: how many turns of it have been played
x: x of one of your points, in mm (points in the order of the statement)
y: y of that point, in mm
vx: horizontal speed of that point, in mm/s
vy: vertical speed of that point, in mm/s
oppX: x of one of your opponent's points
oppY: y of that point
oppVx: horizontal speed of that point
oppVy: vertical speed of that point
angle: angle of one of your joints, in degrees (joints in the order of the statement)
speed: how fast that joint turns, in degrees per second
target: target angle of its motor, in degrees
strength: strength of its motor, in %
oppAngle: angle of one of your opponent's joints
oppSpeed: how fast it turns
oppTarget: target angle of its motor
oppStrength: strength of its motor
myRise: how deep you are ducked, in quarters (0 to 4)
oppRise: how deep your opponent is ducked
myRound: 1 when your lead leg went around the opponent's body last turn (round kick), else 0
oppRound: the same for your opponent
sinceContact: turns since the fighters last touched (0: during the last turn, 21 at most)
sinceHit: turns since a strike last took HP (0: during the last turn, 21 at most)
apart: physics steps since a striking part last touched the other fighter (0 to 10): your lead glove, rear glove, lead foot, rear foot, lead knee, rear knee, then your opponent's

OUTPUT
A command: a move (STANCE, GUARD, RELAX, DUCK, JAB, CROSS, OVERHAND, KICK, HIGH_KICK, STEP_FORWARD, STEP_BACK, JUMP), WAIT, or JOINTS from the second league; optionally followed by a message.
